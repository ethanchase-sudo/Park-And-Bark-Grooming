Meet your groomer

Skilled in every breed, soft on every pup.

Park and Bark Grooming is run by Aissa Goucher, serving Rosamond and the wider Antelope Valley. We cater to dogs of all breeds and sizes in a safe and friendly environment — professional, convenient and affordable.

We work to your specifications and use high quality products, from removing knots and dead hair to stimulating the skin for healthy circulation, ear cleaning, mouth washing, tear stain treatments, nail trimming and full body haircuts.

“Whether it's a quick wash and dry, or a full groom and pampering session, we'll give your pooch the royal treatment.”
